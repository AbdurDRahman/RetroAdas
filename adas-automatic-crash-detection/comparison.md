How to read it:

- *severe missed* is the number that matters most for an emergency-call feature: each one is a violent crash where nobody would be called.
- *PVS false alarms* is the honest real-road number: 3 h of ordinary driving in three cars over asphalt, cobblestone, dirt and speed bumps. Every alarm there is false.
- A rule that looks fine on VZCrash (which only contains pre-filtered harsh events) can still be unusable on real roads if it fires on cobblestone or potholes; that is what the last two columns show.
